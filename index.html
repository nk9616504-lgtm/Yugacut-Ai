About YugaCut Studio — Free No-Watermark Video Editor

YugaCut Studio (yugacut.fixrservice.in) is a fast, browser-based multi-track video editor built for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and WhatsApp Status creators. Edit videos and photo slideshows directly on your phone or PC with zero watermarks and privacy-first local processing.

Key Editing Capabilities: Zero-gap clip joining, 16:9 to 9:16 Zoom & Fill, Pro 5-Tab Text Editor with Tamil & English fonts, Press-and-Hold Live Effects, Chroma Key green-screen removal, Featured Royalty-Free Background Music with live waveform trimming, AI Speech-to-Text captions, real Beat Detection, 1-Tap Auto Reel Maker, and 480p/720p/1080p audio-mixed export.
